Privacy Policy — One Camera

Effective 9 September 2026

One Camera is a camera app published by Blinqdroid. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is short because the app does very little with it.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

One Camera never uploads, copies, or transmits the pictures or video you take. Everything you capture is written to your device's own camera folder (DCIM/Camera), alongside anything your phone's built-in camera has taken, and is yours alone. No image or video data of any kind is sent anywhere.

We have no servers that hold your media, no cloud backup, and no way to see what you shoot.

What the app does collect

One Camera sends diagnostic reports so that faults can be found and fixed. Each report contains:

  • A random installation identifier. A random value created the first time the app runs with diagnostics on, and kept on your device. It is specific to this app: it is not your Google Advertising ID, not your Android device ID, and cannot be used to recognise you in any other app or service. Uninstalling One Camera destroys it, and reinstalling creates a new one.
  • A session identifier distinguishing one run of the app from another.
  • Device information: manufacturer, model, device name, and Android version.
  • App information: the app's name and version.
  • Fault reports: error messages and technical stack traces, including reports of crashes.
  • Capture diagnostics: which camera was used, which processing path a photo took, how many frames a multi-frame merge combined, and how long it took.

Reports also carry the time they were recorded.

This is the complete list. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, accounts, location, or the contents of anything you capture.

What the app does not do

  • No location. The app requests no location permission and records no location. Your photos are not geotagged by One Camera.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising or marketing SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. Nothing profiles your behaviour or follows you between apps.
  • No account. You never sign in, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No data selling or sharing. Collected data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

Purchases

One Camera Pro is a single one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are entered into Google Play and are never seen by, sent to, or stored by One Camera or Blinqdroid. The app asks Google Play whether a purchase exists and receives only a yes or no.

The seven-day trial is tracked by a timestamp stored on your own device. Nothing about the trial is sent anywhere.

Permissions and why they are needed

MicrophoneTo record audio with video.
InternetTo send the diagnostic reports described above.
Storage (Android 9 and older only)To save your photos and videos to the gallery. Android 10 and newer need no storage permission.

Where diagnostic data goes and how long it is kept

Diagnostic reports are sent over an encrypted connection to a collection service operated on behalf of Blinqdroid and used only to diagnose faults in the app. They are retained for no longer than 90 days and then deleted.

Turning diagnostics off

Diagnostic reporting is on by default and you can switch it off at any time: open Settings in the app and turn off Send anonymous diagnostics under Privacy.

Switching it off stops all reporting immediately, discards anything not yet sent, and deletes the installation identifier from your device. If you later switch it back on, a new random identifier is created — it is not the old one, so reports from before and after cannot be connected.

Your rights

You can ask what diagnostic data is associated with your installation, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its collection, by writing to blinqdroid@gmail.com. Because the installation identifier is random and held only on your device, please include it if you can — the app cannot be traced back to you any other way, so without it we may be unable to find the matching records. Uninstalling the app stops all collection immediately and permanently.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your personal data, including under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act. Requests can be sent to the same address.

Children

One Camera is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
