Privacy Policy — One Camera

Effective 9 September 2026

One Camera is a camera app published by Blinqdroid. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is short because the app does not collect any.

One Camera collects nothing

The app gathers no personal data, sends nothing to us, and has no servers to send it to. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

This is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. There is no data.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

One Camera never uploads, copies, or transmits the pictures or video you take. Everything you capture is written to your device's own camera folder (DCIM/Camera), alongside anything your phone's built-in camera has taken, and is yours alone.

We have no way to see what you shoot.

What the app does not do

  • No location. The app requests no location permission and records no location. Your photos are not geotagged by One Camera.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising or marketing SDKs.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. Nothing is measured, recorded or sent — not even anonymously, and not even when the app fails.
  • No account. You never sign in, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No identifiers. The app does not read your advertising ID or your Android device ID, and does not create an identifier of its own.
  • No data selling or sharing. There is nothing to sell or share.

Purchases

One Camera Pro is a single one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are entered into Google Play and are never seen by, sent to, or stored by One Camera or Blinqdroid. The app asks Google Play whether a purchase exists and receives only a yes or no.

Google's own handling of that transaction is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The seven-day trial is tracked by a timestamp stored on your own device. Nothing about the trial is sent anywhere.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy

InternetRequired by the Google Play Billing library to check your purchase. One Camera itself makes no network requests.
Network stateAlso from the billing library: it checks whether you are online before contacting the Play Store.

Internet and network access come with Google's billing library rather than from anything One Camera does. They are listed here because they appear on the app's Play Store listing and you are entitled to know why.

Your rights

Because the app collects nothing, there is no personal data of yours to access, correct, export or delete, and no request you need to make of us. Uninstalling the app removes it and its settings from your device.

If you have a question about this policy, write to blinqdroid@gmail.com.

Children

One Camera is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.
